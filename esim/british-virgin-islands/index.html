Common questions

Do British Virgin Islands eSIM plans include calls and SMS?

6 of the 39 plans we list include calls or SMS. The rest are data-only. Use the Calls icon to filter.

Can I use a hotspot in British Virgin Islands?

23 plans say hotspot or tethering is allowed. Others don’t say, so the Hotspot filter only keeps the ones that do.

When does a British Virgin Islands eSIM activate?

15 plans start when the eSIM first joins a local network, not when you pay. Install on Wi-Fi before you fly.

Does my phone support eSIM?

iPhone XR and later support eSIM, except models sold in mainland China. Many recent Galaxy and Pixel phones do too, if they are unlocked. Use the compatibility checker and look for Add eSIM in settings.
