Common questions

Do Portugal eSIM plans include calls and SMS?

None of the Portugal plans in this import include a phone number, calls, or SMS. Messaging apps use the data allowance.

Can I use a hotspot in Portugal?

The plans we checked for Portugal do not state that hotspot is allowed. Don’t assume tethering works until the provider says so.

When does a Portugal eSIM activate?

Activation rules aren’t stated on every plan. Install on Wi-Fi, then turn on data roaming only for the travel line after you land.

Does my phone support eSIM?

iPhone XR and later support eSIM, except models sold in mainland China. Many recent Galaxy and Pixel phones do too, if they are unlocked. Use the compatibility checker and look for Add eSIM in settings.
