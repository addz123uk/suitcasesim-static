Common questions

Do Sierra Leone eSIM plans include calls and SMS?

6 of the 48 plans we list include calls or SMS. The rest are data-only. Use the Calls icon to filter.

Can I use a hotspot in Sierra Leone?

27 plans say hotspot or tethering is allowed. Others don’t say, so the Hotspot filter only keeps the ones that do.

When does a Sierra Leone eSIM activate?

19 plans start when the eSIM first joins a local network, not when you pay. Install on Wi-Fi before you fly.

Does my phone support eSIM?

iPhone XR and later support eSIM, except models sold in mainland China. Many recent Galaxy and Pixel phones do too, if they are unlocked. Use the compatibility checker and look for Add eSIM in settings.
