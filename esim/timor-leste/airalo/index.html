Common questions

Do Timor-Leste eSIM plans include calls and SMS?

6 of the 16 plans we list include calls or SMS. The rest are data-only. Use the Calls icon to filter.

Can I use a hotspot in Timor-Leste?

The plans we checked for Timor-Leste do not state that hotspot is allowed. Don’t assume tethering works until the provider says so.

When does a Timor-Leste eSIM activate?

16 plans start when the eSIM first joins a local network, not when you pay. Install on Wi-Fi before you fly.

Does my phone support eSIM?

iPhone XR and later support eSIM, except models sold in mainland China. Many recent Galaxy and Pixel phones do too, if they are unlocked. Use the compatibility checker and look for Add eSIM in settings.
